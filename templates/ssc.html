The link didn't work? Save the page and upload it

The SSC response sheet site does not let other websites read the page straight from the link, so if the link doesn't work, save the page and upload it here. It takes a few seconds:

  1. Android (Chrome): open your link, tap ⋮ › Share › Print › Save as PDF, then upload that PDF here.
  2. iPhone (Safari): open your link, tap Share › Print, pinch out on the preview, tap Share › Save to Files, then upload it here.
  3. Computer: open your link, press Ctrl+P (⌘P on Mac) › Save as PDF, or Ctrl+S to save the page, then upload the file here.

On a computer, one click: drag this button to your bookmarks bar, open your response sheet, then click the bookmark. Your sheet opens here with the marks.

Questions

Which exams does this work for?

Any SSC exam whose response sheet comes from the TCS site (ssc.digialm.com): CGL, CHSL, MTS and Havaldar, GD Constable, CPO, JE, Stenographer, Selection Post and others. Pick your exam and the marks are set for you, or type your own.

Is my response sheet sent anywhere?

No. The page reads your response sheet inside your browser and works out the marks there. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Why are my marks different from the official result?

SSC may change the answer key, drop questions, and normalise marks across shifts. This page uses the answers shown in your response sheet, so it is an estimate.
