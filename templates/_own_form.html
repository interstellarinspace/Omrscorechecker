{# The self-service checker: the student's own key, marking scheme and sheet. Run by static/own-key.js. #}

Check your marks with any answer key

Add the answer key, set the marking scheme, then add your OMR sheet. Everything is worked out inside your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1Answer key

Answers can be 1 to 5 or A to E. Write X for a dropped question (everyone gets its marks), and 2/3 when two answers are accepted.

2Marking scheme

Different marks for some questions

One line per range. Questions not listed get the marks for each correct answer.

3Your answers

Photo reading works for the KEA OMR sheet (up to 100 questions, options 1 to 5). For any other sheet, type your answers.
