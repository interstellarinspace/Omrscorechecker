How it works

How do I check my score from my OMR sheet?

Choose your subject, take a clear photo of your OMR answer sheet (candidate copy) with both side edges visible, and upload it. The app reads your Question Booklet Version Code, Register Number and every answer, then scores it against the answer key for your version.

What is the marking scheme?

1 mark for each correct answer. Option 5 (not attempted) gets 0. 0.25 marks are deducted for each wrong answer, for a question with no bubble shaded, and for a question with more than one bubble shaded.

Which answer key is used?

The provisional answer keys published by the Karnataka Examinations Authority on 22.11.2026 for Paper I General Knowledge and Paper II Kannada and Science. The final keys and official results announced by KEA are what count.
