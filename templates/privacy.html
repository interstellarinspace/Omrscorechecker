Privacy policy

This policy explains what information {{ cfg.site_name }} (the website and the phone app) uses, why, and the choices you have. {{ cfg.site_name }} is an independent tool to help candidates check OMR answer sheets and calculate marks. It is not run by, or connected with, any exam authority or government body.

Information you give us

{% if static_site %}

Answer sheet photos stay on your device. On this website, the photo or PDF you choose is read and scored inside your own browser. It is not uploaded to us, and we do not store the photo, your answers or your marks.

{% endif %}

Published results. Where an administrator publishes results on this site, each result is stored in encrypted form and can be opened only by entering the candidate's details on the Results page. There is no public list of results.

Phone app. When you check a sheet in the app, the photo you choose, the subject you pick and the name you type (optional) are sent to our server to read the sheet and return your score. The app uses the camera only when you tap to take a photo.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services below may receive standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device, the pages you open and the time of your visit. Some page code (for example the scanner and PDF tools) is loaded from public content delivery networks such as jsDelivr and cdnjs, which receive the same kind of request information.

Advertisers never see your answer sheet photo, your answers or your marks.

How we use information

We use information only to run the site and app, calculate and show scores, keep the service secure, understand how it is used, and show ads that keep it free. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

This service is meant for exam candidates and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
